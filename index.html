Focus: Own Your Time

Privacy & Terms

1. 100% On-Device Privacy

Focus was engineered with a strict, zero-tracking architecture. We believe that your schedule, routines, and deep-work analytics are your property. Focus does not collect, harvest, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers.

2. Data Storage & iCloud Sync

All application data is managed locally on your physical device using Apple's native SwiftData framework. If you unlock Focus Pro and enable Multi-Device Sync, your data is routed exclusively through your personal Apple iCloud account via CloudKit.

  • Your data is stored in a private, encrypted iCloud database zone.
  • The developer has absolutely zero access to this database.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs are installed in this application.

3. Apple Calendar Integration

If you choose to enable Calendar Routing, Focus will request permission to read and write to your local Apple Calendar via EventKit. This operation happens entirely on your device. Your calendar events are never transmitted to us or any third parties.
